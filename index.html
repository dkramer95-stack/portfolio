About

I am an accountant by training — tax, revenue recognition, bookkeeping — who moved into empirical research on technology and labour markets. I live in Jakarta and work in US Eastern hours.

The through-line in the work above is measurement discipline: separating what a model can do from what a firm did, and stating in advance what would prove the argument wrong. Three of the papers report results that undercut their own opening hypothesis, which I take to be the point.

Available for research, data analysis, and remote accounting work. The code behind every artifact is public.
